About me

Sveta Dvipa is more than a holistic health centre—it is the culmination of a lifelong journey rooted in ancient traditions and personal awakening. For generations, my family has practiced Vedic rituals and healing arts, passing down the wisdom of the Vedas and natural healing. Growing up in this sacred lineage, I began practicing yoga at an early age, which laid the foundation for a path that would later lead me to the profound teachings of Ayurveda, Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), and Daoism.

Languages and mythology have always been my passion. As a linguist, I have explored Greek, Slavo-Aryan, and Germanic traditions, seeking deeper meaning in their myths, symbols, and sacred texts.

Through practices such as yoga nidra, etymology, and Runic spells (Galdr), I began to awaken my ancestral memory, reconnecting with the ancient wisdom that flows through my blood.

These practices, encoded in language, movement, and sound, revealed the path of remembrance and inner alchemy—a path I now share through Sveta Dvipa.
